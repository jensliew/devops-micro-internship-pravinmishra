Week 1 Reflection, DMI Campus (a little late, but here)

The past couple of weeks have honestly been packed for me, work, personal stuff, all of it stacking up, which is exactly why this Week 1 post is coming out later than it should have. Still wanted to get it out properly instead of rushing a half version just to be on time.

Just wrapped up week one of the DevOps Micro Internship (DMI), Campus track, and honestly this week hit different than I expected. I thought it'd be all technical stuff, but most of what stuck with me was more about how my own head works.

Big one for me: I keep noticing that stuff I do now, like reading or just being disciplined about something, doesn't show any results right away. For a long time I think I low-key assumed that meant it wasn't working. This week made me realize that's just wrong. The payoff is delayed, not absent. I'm someone who trusts the process even without seeing proof yet, and I didn't fully clock that about myself until now.

Also something I'm not proud of: I get nervous and anxious pretty easily when things start piling up, and it messes with my focus more than I'd like to admit. Working out daily actually helps a lot with that, it generates endorphins naturally and calms me down when my head's all over the place. That's the thing I actually want to fix this year, staying calm and just checking things off one by one instead of spiraling about everything at once.

This week I mostly worked on figuring out where my time actually goes, tracking progress instead of just doing stuff and forgetting about it, and trying to do one thing at a time instead of half doing five things.

Going forward: Saturdays and Sundays, 9am to 11am, phone in another room, one task. Not trying to be productive overnight, just trying to build something I'll actually stick with for the next 5 months, delays and all.

Onto week 2.
